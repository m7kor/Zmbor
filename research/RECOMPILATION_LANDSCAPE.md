# Recompilation landscape

2026-11-07 research snapshot. The [technical matrix](COMPETITOR_TECHNICAL_MATRIX.md) contains six deeper comparisons. The table below records a focused source check per additional project. Push dates and licenses follow repository metadata and are used for triage. They can differ from the date or licensing of the individual file. Architecture and code observations are source-based, proposed uses are inferred, and independent compatibility and performance remain unknown.
